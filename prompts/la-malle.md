# LA MALLE: luxury advent calendar (Route A, original maison): prompt sheet

## Shot bible (Gate 1), from docs/luxury-advent-analysis.md §3
- **Hero trunk, LOCKED:** Higgsfield image job `eded20be-c395-4737-84b8-639232b93de0` (option T3).
  Pass it as an image reference in **every** shot so the trunk never changes.
  Ivory lacquer, dove-grey panels, champagne-gold trim, an oval mirror in a lid that opens up, 24 drawers
  (6 columns × 4 rows, split 3 + 3 around a central gown alcove, numbered 1–24 left to right, row by row),
  a snowy gingerbread village under glass in the base, and a front plate "N° 017 / 170".
- **Location:** an interior Paris salon at winter dusk, with warm lamps. The window shows Paris rooftops in snow at blue hour.
  **No Eiffel Tower** (cliché), no night city, no neon.
- **Hands only, no faces.** Drawers only slide straight toward the camera (use an end frame).
  Numbers face the camera. Nothing with text on it rotates. No brand wordmarks.
- **Video:** Kling 3.0 **4K** for reveal shots (30 credits), Pro for the drawer and detail shots (7.5).

## Rejected options
- T4: drawer numbers wrong (009, 010, 021…) and an Eiffel Tower in the window.
- T1/T2: good furniture, but no numbered plate and the numbers can't be read.
