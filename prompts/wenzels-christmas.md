# Wenzel's The Bakers: 5 Christmas product videos (shot plan)
_Request via EPL Performance · @wenzelsthebakers (18.2K) · 9:16 · ~12–14s each · gingerbread theme_

All details are taken from Wenzel's own 12 Nov 2026 Christmas reel and profile. **Products, prices, store and packaging are real.**
Their reels get 1–5K plays but only around 3 likes each, so polished, satisfying Christmas content would stand out on their page → a good chance of a repost.

## Shot bible (Gate 1): all 5 videos
- **Store interior (real Wenzel's look):** charcoal-grey walls with **orange geometric accent panels**, exposed brick-style tiling,
  black metal multi-tier shelving with wicker baskets, a **charcoal stacked-brick counter front**, light oak plank floor, a black ceiling
  grid with warm-white LED downlights, and **digital menu boards** in black frames with orange branding above the counter.
- **Christmas dressing (add):** a small real Christmas tree with warm white lights, pine garland along the shelving, red baubles, a few
  gingerbread-man decorations. Tasteful, not overloaded.
- **Logo:** **"Wenzel's"** in a **bold rounded retro script, white on vivid orange (#FF5500)**, with "the bakers" in lowercase sans-serif
  beneath on the signage. It appears on: an **illuminated wall sign** behind the counter (soft-focus background) and the **paper bag**.
  ⚠️ AI may warp the script, so check every image. If it does, I fix it with an image-edit pass using their real logo
  (`assets/wenzels/logo_ref.jpg`).
- **Bag:** ⚠️ **design not verified.** Default: an **orange paper takeaway bag with the white "Wenzel's" script**. **Ask EPL/Wenzel's for a photo of the real bag.**
- **Plate / tray:** Wenzel's use **high-gloss black rectangular trays with rounded corners**. Use those (the brief said "plate": a round black plate is the alternative).
- **Hands:** a staff member's hands in **black food-prep gloves** and a black uniform sleeve. **No faces.**
- **Making happens** at the **finishing station behind the store counter**, so we stay "inside a Wenzel's store" throughout.
- **One action per shot**, slow motion, real food physics: drips fall **down**, piping moves in **one direction**, dust falls **down**.
- **Look:** `photoreal food commercial, ARRI Alexa 35, 100mm macro, Kodak Vision3 250D, warm key light with soft fill, shallow depth of field,
  real glaze sheen, crumbs and sugar texture, slight imperfections, slow motion 120fps feel, no CGI sheen`
- **Sound:** Kling sound on for food ASMR (drizzle, crunch, sprinkles), plus festive music added in-app.

## Each video: 4 shots, ~13s
1. **Make (0–4s):** macro of the finishing touch happening (the hero ASMR moment)
2. **Detail (4–7s):** extreme macro of texture (drip settling, sugar sparkle)
3. **Plate + bag (7–11s):** the product on a black tray on the counter, **Wenzel's bag beside it**, the store and **lit Wenzel's sign** soft behind
4. **End (11–13s):** text overlay **product name + real price**, then "DREAMREACH."
