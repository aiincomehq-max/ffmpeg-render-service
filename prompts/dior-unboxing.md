# "The £6,500 Dior advent calendar unboxing": shot plan
_Gloved hands only · luxury home kitchen · Christmas · 9:16 · ~30s · reference: Dior's own product photo (uploaded to Higgsfield)_

## ⚠️ Honesty and brand (decided before generating)
- This is **AI-generated** footage of a real £6,500 product. The bottles, labels and details **will not match the real thing exactly**.
  Presenting it as a real unboxing would mislead people thinking of buying it. So:
  - **On-screen title:** `The £6,500 Dior advent calendar unboxing`. The first caption line reads **"AI-imagined unboxing ✨"**, and the
    platform **AI label is on** for every post.
  - No claim to own it, no affiliate links, no "link in bio to buy".
- "Dior" is used only to say what the product is (title and caption). **No Dior wordmark is generated by the AI**: the title is a text overlay.
- Dior/LVMH could still ask for it to come down. Low risk with honest labelling, but not zero.

## Shot bible (Gate 1)
- **The cabinet (match the reference photo):** glossy white lacquer, **two side wings that swing outward** on gold hinges, gold
  **bow/candy-shaped drawer handles**, a gold clasp and handles on the outside, a **top section that lifts up** to show a candle and a
  **snow globe**, and a central **gingerbread alcove** with a miniature gingerbread gown, chair and tiny gift boxes behind gold rope trim.
  About 70cm tall: a large piece, so full-size 100ml bottles **do** fit in its drawers.
- **Location:** a luxury home kitchen in the **evening**, interior. A **white marble island** with the marble front facing the camera,
  pale oak or cream cabinetry, warm under-cabinet lights.
- **Background (soft focus):** a decorated Christmas tree with warm white lights, a garland over a range hood or shelf, candles, a couple of
  wrapped gifts. **No windows with city views. No people, no faces.**
- **Hands:** a **woman's hands in fitted ivory satin gloves**, slim and elegant, with natural movement. Never more than two hands.
- **One direction per shot:** wings swing **outward**, drawers slide **toward camera**, the top lifts **upward**. Each pinned with an end frame.
- **Text risks:** the bottle labels (Collection Privée) **will garble**, so keep labels at an angle or soft. No readable label in sharp focus.
- **Look:** `photoreal luxury product film, ARRI Alexa 35, 50mm and 100mm macro, Kodak Vision3 250D, warm tungsten practicals,
  shallow depth of field, real lacquer reflections, fingerprints-free gloss, soft dust in the light, no CGI sheen`
- **Sound:** Kling sound **on** for real ASMR foley (clasp click, drawer slide, glass clink), plus soft music added in-app.

**What this means for the video:** it's a **floor-standing piece of furniture about 1.1 m tall**, not a box for the worktop. On a kitchen island it would
nearly touch the ceiling. Our shots made it island-sized (~60 cm), which made the full-size bottles look oversized in the drawers.
**Fix:** the cabinet stands **on the kitchen floor in front of the marble island**, with the marble front behind and beside it. Bottles about as tall as a drawer front.
