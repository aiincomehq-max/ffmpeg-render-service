## ⚠️ Decide before generating
1. **What is it for?**
   - **If it's an ad for a real pair someone is selling:** AI won't reproduce *that* pair's exact Himalaya scale pattern
     and colouring, so a buyer would be shown a product that isn't the one they get. For a sale, **film the real pair**
     (I can shoot-plan it for a phone and grade it to look premium), or label it clearly as an AI visual.
   - **If it's content or a hook** ("what a £X sandal unboxing looks like"): fine with the **AI label on** and "AI visual" in the caption.
2. **The price on the title card must be real.** The 1stDibs screenshot doesn't show a price. For the Birkin example, a
   "£500,000" figure needs checking: Himalaya Birkins usually sell for far less, apart from rare auction records.
   **Supply the real figure, or I'll leave it off.**
3. **Hermès risk:** the Chypre's "H" strap *is* the trademarked design. Hermès won the MetaBirkins case (2023) over
   AI/digital images of its bags. Labelled, non-commercial content is lower risk; using it in a sales ad for something that isn't real stock is the risky case.

## Shot bible (Gate 1)
- **The sandal (match the reference):** a men's slide with a **black anatomical footbed** and a black lugged sole. A **wide
  crossover strap in the "H" cut-out shape**, plus a separate **velcro-style ankle strap**. **Himalaya crocodile**:
  white fading into smoky grey-brown, with large glossy scales. **One pair**, the same pair in every shot.
- **Location:** a **luxury boutique stockroom**, interior, evening. Floor-to-ceiling **shelves of stacked orange gift boxes**,
  soft overhead spots, a **dark walnut or leather-topped unboxing table**. **No windows, no shop floor, no customers, no faces.**
- **Packaging:** an orange box with a brown ribbon, a cotton dust bag, tissue paper. **No AI-generated "HERMÈS" wordmark**:
  boxes turned so the lid text isn't readable, or shown plain.
- **Hands:** a **woman's hands in fitted ivory satin gloves**, two hands maximum, with natural movement.
- **One direction per shot:** the ribbon pulls **outward**, the lid lifts **up**, the sandal is lifted **out and toward camera**.
- **Text risk:** the embossed "H" on the insole may warp. Keep it soft or angled.
- **Look:** `photoreal luxury product film, ARRI Alexa 35, 100mm macro and 50mm, Kodak Vision3 250D, warm overhead spot
  plus soft fill, shallow depth of field, real crocodile scale texture with natural sheen and fine creases, no CGI sheen`
- **Sound:** Kling sound on for ASMR foley (ribbon, tissue rustle, lid).
