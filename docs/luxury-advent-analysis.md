## 2. Why it's worth £6,500: the lessons for our videos
The products alone don't justify the price. **The object and the story do.** That's what our videos need to copy:

1. **The container is the star.** It's a piece of furniture you keep, not a box you open. → Our series needs **one hero object** that appears in every episode, and the Day 0 teaser is just that object.
2. **A real piece of the brand's history at the centre.** Dior uses a 1949 gown and the medallion chair, an icon of the house. → Each episode links its object to a piece of the house's history, not just "here's a perfume".
3. **Scarcity is part of the story.** 170 numbered pieces. → Show a brass plate engraved "N° 017 / 170". It costs nothing and makes it feel collectable.
4. **Theatre, not just products.** A gingerbread scene, a snow globe, a tree-topper star. → Mix in "wonder" days (snow globe, ornaments, the star), not only beauty products.
5. **A rhythm of big items and small delights.** 6 hero fragrances spread across 24 days, with small pleasures in between. → Put the hero items on the days people engage most: Day 1, the weekends, Day 24.
6. **It becomes part of the room.** The trunk turns into a dressing table. → The finale shows it in a home, lit for Christmas, not on a studio bench.

## 3. The video plan: "LA MALLE" format
**Route still to decide** (see `hermes-advent-plan.md` §0). For a named luxury house, **the brand-risk rules apply in the same way.**
Dior is LVMH-owned and also protects its marks. A spec film showing an AI version of a **real product they sell** could
also mislead buyers, because the AI items won't match exactly. Recommended:
- **Route A:** our own maison, using this exact structure, with original objects. A portfolio piece.
- **Route B:** pitch the format to a brand that reposts. **The most likely way to get a repost.**
- **Route C:** a named brand, labelled unofficial. High risk, with no product shown as if it were the real item.

### Shot bible (Gate 1): all episodes
- **Hero object:** a miniature couture **dressing-table trunk**: ivory lacquer, **grey and gold details**, an oval mirror, **24 small drawers** with brass numbers, a tiny couture gown on a stand in the centre, a gingerbread-and-snow diorama in the base. **One approved hero still is locked and used as a reference in every episode.**
- **Location:** an interior Paris apartment salon, **winter dusk**, warm lamplight. A tall window shows **Paris rooftops in snow at blue hour**: never night city, neon or modern towers.
- **Hands only:** gloved or bare, elegant. **No faces.**
- **Drawers only slide straight toward the camera**, with an end frame pinning "fully open". **Numbers always face the camera**, and nothing with text on it rotates.
- **No brand wordmarks generated by AI.** Any brand name is added as a text overlay in the edit.
- **Camera standard** (paste into every prompt): `photoreal luxury film still, ARRI Alexa 35, 100mm macro, Kodak Vision3 250D, warm tungsten lamplight against blue-hour window light, shallow depth of field, real material texture (lacquer, brass, silk, glass), soft dust in the light, quiet and expensive, no CGI sheen, no text`
- **Grade:** ivory, dove grey, champagne gold. Cool blue window light against warm lamps.
- **Music:** one slow waltz or piano piece across all 24 (royalty-free or in-app). Light chimes on the drawer opens.

### Episode template (8s): 3 shots, 3 clips
1. **0–2s, the drawer:** macro of a gloved hand pulling drawer N straight out toward the camera (end frame = fully open).
2. **2–5.5s, the reveal:** the object in the drawer. A slow push-in, light catching glass, silk or metal.
3. **5.5–8s, the wonder detail:** an extreme macro of something magical: perfume mist in lamplight, snow settling in the globe, silk falling.
- **Overlay:** "DAY N / 24" + "DREAMREACH."

### Day 0 teaser (15s, posted 30 Nov): the trunk itself
