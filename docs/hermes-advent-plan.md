## 0. Read first: legal risk (this decides the whole plan)
Hermès defends its trademarks harder than almost any brand. In **Hermès v. Rothschild (2023)** a US jury found the
"MetaBirkins" digital artworks infringed Hermès trademarks and awarded damages. That case was about digital
images of Birkin bags, which is close to what an AI advent calendar of Hermès products would be.

What that means for us:
- **Expect zero reposts and a real chance of a takedown or cease-and-desist** if it's posted using Hermès names, logos and product designs.
- **Never sell anything** off the back of it: no merch, no paid placement, no "Hermès" in a handle or hashtag that suggests affiliation.

**Pick a route before production:**

| Route | What it is | Reposts? | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Hermès-inspired "Maison" calendar (recommended)** | The same high-end format and orange-and-saddle-leather mood, but **no Hermès name, logo, H, or recognisable bag designs** (no Birkin, Kelly or Constance shapes). Original objects in the same spirit | It's a portfolio piece that luxury brands can see you're capable of | Low |
| **B. Pitch version for a brand that would repost** | Same format, made for a **smaller luxury house** (UK leather goods, perfume, jewellery) that reposts creator content | **Yes, the real goal** | Low |
| **C. Hermès, unofficial** | As asked: named products, clearly labelled *"unofficial fan concept, not affiliated with Hermès"* | Very unlikely | **High**: takedown / legal letter |

The rest of this plan works for all three. Only the product list and captions change.

## 1. Concept: "24, FAUBOURG"
Hermès' flagship is at **24 rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré**, and its "Mosaïque au 24" tableware is named after it. **24 days = 24 Faubourg.**
(Route A: rename it "**24, RUE DES ARTISANS**" or similar, to keep the idea without the address.)

**The calendar object** appears in every episode and is the series signature: a handmade **advent cabinet of 24 small
saddle-leather drawers**, in a **Hermès-orange** lacquered frame, hand-stitched, with brass number plates. It stands on a walnut
workbench in a Paris atelier with tall windows and soft winter daylight.

**Each episode, 7–9s:**
1. **Hook (0–1.5s):** macro shot of a gloved artisan's hand pulling drawer **[N]** open toward the camera.
2. **Reveal (1.5–5s):** the object for that day rises into the light. Slow dolly-in, rack focus, real reflections.
3. **Craft detail (5–7.5s):** one extreme macro of how it's made: saddle stitch, silk-screen ink, enamel being polished.
4. **Sign-off (7.5–9s):** the drawer closes; small text "**DAY N / 24**", then "**DREAMREACH.**"

**Day 24** is a 20s finale: the camera pulls back from the cabinet with all 24 drawers open, ending on snowfall outside the atelier window.

## 2. Shot bible (Gate 1): applies to every episode
- **Location:** an interior Paris leather atelier, daytime, December. Tall arched windows showing **soft overcast winter daylight and Paris rooftops in light snow** (never night, never a modern city). Warm practical lamps only.
- **Through the windows:** Paris zinc rooftops, light snow, overcast sky. **Never** traffic, neon or skyscrapers.
- **Surfaces:** walnut workbench, worn leather offcuts, linen, real tools (awls, waxed thread, edge paint). Nothing pristine or CGI-clean.
- **Hands only:** artisan hands in fitted white cotton gloves or bare, weathered craftsman hands. **No faces, ever.**
- **The cabinet:** always the **same design** (24 drawers in a 6×4 grid, orange frame, saddle-tan drawers, brass plates). Lock it with one approved hero still and pass that image as a reference to every episode.
- **Numbers:** engraved on brass plates and **always facing the camera.** Drawers **only move straight toward camera** (one direction), with an end frame used to pin "drawer fully open".
- **Text risk:** AI misspells brand wordmarks. **Never generate the Hermès wordmark** (Route C adds it as an overlay in the edit or not at all). The only on-screen text is the overlay "DAY N / 24".
- **Rotation risk:** nothing carrying a number or text spins (it would mirror). Turntable shots are for plain objects only.

**Camera and realism standard** (paste into every prompt):
`photoreal luxury product film still, shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with a 100mm macro lens, Kodak Vision3 250D, soft north-window daylight, shallow depth of field, natural dust in the light, real leather grain and stitch texture, subtle imperfections, quiet, expensive, editorial, no CGI sheen, no text`

**Grade:** warm neutral, cream highlights, deep brown shadows, Hermès orange as the **only** saturated colour. Fine grain, no vignette gimmicks.
**Music:** one slow solo piano or string-quartet piece, the same across all 24 (royalty-free, or the IG/TikTok in-app library). Restrained, no beat drops.
