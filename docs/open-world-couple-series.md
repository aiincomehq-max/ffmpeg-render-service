## The rules for this series (why it can't be taken down)
- **Original characters and an original world.** No Lucia, Jason, Rockstar logos, GTA HUD, Vice City map or any recognisable GTA asset.
  The look is an *open-world crime game*, not GTA.
- **Jokes, not facts.** Captions are framed as jokes ("…be like 😂", "If GTA 6 had this…"). **Never** "Did you know you can…" claims, and
  no real-money, bank-transfer or drug-dealing hooks.
- **No real brands** on screen (no Louis Vuitton, Tesla etc.). Original fashion, original cars.
- **AI label on** for every post. "GTA 6" appears in captions only as the subject of the joke.

## Characters (lock as reference images first, then every video uses them)
- **DRE:** late 20s, Black British, short twists, gold chain, white vest under an open Hawaiian shirt, cargo shorts, fresh trainers. Confident, a bit useless in a crisis.
- **MIA:** late 20s, Latina, long dark wavy hair, gold hoops, cropped denim jacket, high-waisted jeans. Sharp, unimpressed, always right.
- **World:** "PALM COVE", an invented sunny neon Florida-style city. Pastel art-deco strip, palm trees, highways, a marina, neon nights.
- **Game feel without copying:** third-person "game camera" angles, a subtle **original** mini-map and mission text in our own font
  (e.g. "MISSION: GET THE HOT WATER BOTTLE"), and a slight video-game render sheen. **No GTA UI imitation.**
