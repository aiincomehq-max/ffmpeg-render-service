# Episode 3 — "I just built this in 20 minutes"

**Archetype:** face to camera, "I just asked ChatGPT/Claude to build me an n8n workflow that does X, it took 20 minutes, it runs itself." Often with a quick screen pan over a canvas of green ticks.
**Length:** 32s.

| Beat | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Claim | Frozen on the canvas of green ticks. | "Twenty minutes. Runs itself. Look at all the green." |
| Mechanism | Circle the green ticks, then the word "just". | "'Just' is the whole trick. It deletes the setup, the credentials, the timezone bug, the failed posts. And green ticks mean the nodes ran. They don't mean anything reached a person." |
| Receipt | Screen recording of the FDHQ log: the tab with 205 publish_failed rows, scrolling. | "This is one tab of one of my logs. Two hundred and five failed publishes. Every one of those runs was green somewhere. A workflow that runs is not a workflow that works." |
| Turn | Card: "Green means it ran. Did anyone see it?" | "Ask what happened after the green. Comment RECEIPTS. Free. No course." |

**Hook patterns:** *The effortless frame* ("I just": the labour is removed from the sentence so the viewer imagines none). *Green-tick proof* (execution shown as if it were outcome).
