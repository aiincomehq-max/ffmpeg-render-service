# Episode 2 — The dashboard screenshot

**Archetype:** a cropped Stripe, Shopify, or creator-fund screenshot with a big number and no date, no account, no period. "AI did this in 30 days."
**Length:** 30s.

| Beat | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Claim | The screenshot frozen, handle blurred. | "Eleven thousand four hundred dollars. Thirty days. A screenshot." |
| Mechanism | Circle the crop edges, the missing date, the missing period selector. | "A screenshot reads as proof, but a crop is a choice. There's no date, no period, no account name. What you're looking at is a rectangle. The rectangle was chosen." |
| Receipt | The FDHQ cost ledger tab, gate cost $2.30 circled, then the OhChat cost tab, $23.40. | "Here's my dashboard. I ledgered every API call to four decimal places. Total spend on the biggest product: twenty-three dollars. Total revenue across all thirty: zero. I'll show you the whole sheet, not the rectangle." |
| Turn | Card: "Where's the edge of the crop?" | "Next time, look for the edge of the crop. Comment RECEIPTS. Free. No course." |

**Hook patterns:** *The chosen rectangle* (a crop that removes every field that would let you check the number). *Dashboard as proof* (a UI element borrowed for credibility, when a UI can show any number).
