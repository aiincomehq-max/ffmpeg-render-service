# The first five Reels

Format: paper cards, handwritten or a handwriting font on textured paper, one card per beat, trending low-key audio, no avatar, no logo. Each card holds one line under 12 words. The first card is on screen inside the first second. Total length 15 to 20 seconds so the loop works on TikTok and Instagram; a 35-second cut for YouTube adds the "receipt" cards marked optional.

Every Reel ends with the same on-screen line and the same caption keyword: **Comment LEDGER and I'll send you the real numbers. Free, no course.** The DM funnel that answers it is in `../funnel/`.

Numbers in square brackets come from the logs. Do not post a card with a bracket in it; fill it or cut it.
