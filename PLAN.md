# What this is for

Working notes from the decisions behind the renderer. Recorded so the reasoning
survives a break. Some of it is judgement rather than fact, marked where so.

## The business, in one line

Publish faceless short-form video about content automation, capture email, and
sell a content engine to people who already ship content volume.

## Why not the obvious version

The instinct is to build an audience first and work out monetisation later.
That is the common failure: the content that earns views and the content that
attracts buyers are usually different, and by the time it becomes clear there
is an audience that will never pay.

Related trap, since the niche is content performance: proving the product
seems to require having gone viral, which requires the product. That circle
stalls people indefinitely.

The way out is that proof does not have to be view counts. Three kinds exist:

- **Results proof.** "My videos got X views." Requires the thing we lack.
- **Borrowed proof.** Decomposing other people's wins. What most accounts in
  this niche actually run on, and it is unfalsifiable.
- **Artifact proof.** The thing works in front of the viewer, now.

Artifact proof is available immediately and almost nobody else in this niche
can offer it, because they cannot build. So the content is the product
running: videos generated by this pipeline, about this pipeline, showing the
input and the render time on screen.

## Buyer

Automation operators who already sell content services. n8n users, small
social shops, people shipping volume for clients.

Not aspiring creators. They are reachable but do not pay, and an audience of
them cannot be converted into revenue. [Judgement, not measured.]

## Price ladder

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Free | £0 | Findings from the scraped dataset, posted daily |
| Entry | £49 | The hook data, cleaned and structured |
| Core | £249 | This render service plus the n8n workflow that drives it |

Pre-sell the core at £99 to the first twenty buyers before it is polished.
Optimise for the date of the first sale, not its size.

## Positioning

The differentiated asset is the scraped performance dataset, not the workflow
library. Workflow packs get copied in a week. A dataset with real outcomes
attached supports claims that can be checked, which is the opposite of the
post-hoc storytelling the rest of the niche runs on.

**Do not resell raw scraped rows.** Redistributing a verbatim platform scrape
likely breaches terms of service and is the kind of thing that gets a product
pulled. Sell derived findings, aggregates and structures. That is also the
better product.

## Order of work

1. Confirm buyer and price. Then stop deciding.
2. Landing page and email capture, before any posting. A viral post with no
   capture is a donation to the platform.
3. First twenty posts, each one a finding from the dataset, rendered by this
   pipeline.
4. Measure opt-ins per thousand views. Not likes, not follows. Kill posts on
   that number.
