# Posting the text-block set

Five silent ~5.5s verticals in `build-text/`. Built to the spec taken off the
one faceless in-niche outlier, @chinglung._.p — 1M views on 4,700 followers.

| file | angle | words |
|---|---|---|
| `1-gone-quiet.mp4` | he withdrew; it is fear, not indifference | 115 |
| `2-keeps-coming-back.mp4` | the one who leaves and returns | 118 |

## The one step that is not optional

**Add the music inside the Instagram app, at post time.** These ship silent on
purpose.

- Baking a commercial track into the file gets the post muted or taken down.
  In-app audio is licensed, and using it is part of why these travel.
- Pick something slow, vintage and widely recognised. The reference post used
  *500 Miles* — the melancholic-familiar register is the point, not that
  specific song.

## What is deliberately absent

- **No call to action.** Not in the video, not in the caption. The reference has
  none, and it did 1M. Every instinct from the previous five videos said to end
  on "comment your number" — that pattern came from the talking-head posts, not
  this one. Do not add it.
- **No voiceover.** It is read, not listened to.
- **No movement.** All text is on screen from the first frame and never changes.

## Why 5.5 seconds for 110 words

The clip is far shorter than the text takes to read. That is the mechanic. The
viewer replays or pauses, and the loop is what moves it. It is not an error and
it should not be "fixed" by lengthening the video.

## Captions

Keep them near-empty, as the reference does.

```
For entertainment purposes only.
#soulmate #manifestation #tarot #fyp
```

## Cadence and kill rule

One a day for five days on `dreamreach.x`. **If none clears 10,000 views, stop
and do not make more of these.**

The honest caveat: @chinglung._.p has a 98,700-view median on 4,700 followers —
that account was already warm, and its 1M post is only 10.5× its own median, the
lowest multiple in the whole sample. Every genuine standing-start breakout we
found is a human on camera (@_austinbecker_: 1,800-view median → 846K, 458×).
So this format is unproven from a cold start. These five are the cheap test of
whether that matters.

## Background and text scale, measured off the reference

The first attempt looked like a designed quote card. Two measurable reasons,
both now fixed:

- **The background was a drawn gradient, not a photograph.** It had no room in
  it. The plate now used is a flat phone-camera shot of a plain wall: ceiling
  line across the upper left, the back of a head and a shoulder cropped into
  the bottom-left corner, soft daylight from the right. The subject starts 59%
  down the frame; the reference's starts at 56%.
- **The text was about 45% too big and the column far too wide.** The reference
  runs roughly a 26px body at 1080 wide with a 31px line gap in a ~430px
  column, breaking at six or seven words, with the block spanning 16%–46% of
  frame height. Those are the numbers the build now uses. A wide column at a
  large size is what reads as a graphic.

`plates/wall.png` is the plate in use; `plates/wall-alt.png` is a second take
held in reserve. A real phone photo of a real wall is still a drop-in swap if
one turns up.
