The Face You Keep Almost Remembering

Most of us carry a vague sense of someone we haven't met yet. A psychic soulmate sketch turns that feeling into something you can actually look at.

It usually arrives late at night, or in the gap between one thing and the next. A sense that there is a person out there you would recognise instantly — and no face to put to it.

You can't picture them. You can almost picture them. That gap is oddly uncomfortable, and most people never do anything about it.

A growing number of people have started doing something about it: asking a psychic artist for a personalized digital sketch of the person they're meant to meet.

It isn't a photograph and it doesn't claim to be. It's a reading, rendered as an image — a way of giving shape to something you've only ever felt.

What people tend to say afterwards is not that the sketch was uncanny, but that it was specific. A jawline. An expression. Something about the eyes that felt less like a stranger than it should have.

Whether you take it as insight or as a strange and beautiful object is up to you. Plenty of people buy one as a gift. Plenty buy one for themselves and don't tell anyone.

The reading takes a few questions about you — not a photo, not your personal details — and the sketch follows from there.
